## Core Features

Virexa is built on **powerful features** that work together to create a complete Discord moderation and management solution. Each feature is designed to save your staff time while keeping your community safe and engaged.

---

### Moderation System

Fully structured moderation with case tracking, escalation rules, and detailed analytics.

**Highlights**

- Unique case IDs for every action (warn, mute, kick, ban)  
- Enforced reasons for transparency and accountability  
- Mass actions to quickly handle raids and spam waves  
- History and activity surfaced directly in the dashboard  

[Learn more →](moderation.md)

---

### Backup & Restore

Never lose your server structure again. Virexa focuses on structural backups so you can confidently roll back large changes.

**Highlights**

- Snapshot channels, categories, roles, and key server settings  
- Safe restore workflows to avoid surprises  
- Ideal for migrations, restructures, and disaster recovery  

[Learn more →](backup.md)

---

### Advanced Logging

Detailed logging for key Discord events, sent to your log channels and exposed via the dashboard and API.

**Highlights**

- Logs moderation actions, role changes, and channel updates  
- Feeds into `GET /api/logs` and `WEBSOCKET /ws/logs` for live dashboards  
- Useful for audits, incident reviews, and analytics  

[Learn more →](logging.md)

---

### Role Management

Auto-roles, self-assignable roles, sticky roles, and bulk operations to keep large communities organized.

**Highlights**

- Automatically assign roles when members or bots join  
- Preserve important roles when users leave and rejoin  
- Apply roles in bulk during campaigns or reorganizations  
- All operations respect Discord role hierarchy and safety constraints  

[Learn more →](roles.md)

---

### AFK & Reminders

Keep expectations clear and important follow-ups on track with AFK and reminder tooling.

**Highlights**

- AFK status tracking so mentions don’t go unanswered without context  
- Personal and server-level reminders for tasks and events  
- Integrated with your existing moderation and logging flows  

[Learn more →](afk.md)

---

### Boards & Counters

Visualize community momentum and highlight the best content.

**Highlights**

- Starboard-style channels that promote popular messages  
- Counters for members, boosts, and other metrics  
- Great for milestone tracking and public visibility  

[Learn more →](boards.md)
